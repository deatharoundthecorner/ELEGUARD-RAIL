Report an Elephant Sighting Near the Tracks

Your observation provides critical early warning intelligence to help train crews and wildlife rangers reduce the risk of collisions along railway corridors. Reports undergo independent DWC ecological review before Railway Control evaluates operational advisories.

STEP 1
Observe Safely
Never approach elephants. Maintain a safe observer standoff distance (50–150m) and do not use bright lights or flash.
STEP 2
Note Track Landmark
Identify the nearest railway landmark: milepost, culvert marker, railway bridge, or crossing gate.
STEP 3
Two-Stage Verification
DWC Wildlife Rangers first verify observation data. Next, Railway Operations Control independently assesses track hazards and issues train advisories if necessary. For emergencies, dial 1992.
